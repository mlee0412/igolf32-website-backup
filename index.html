FAQ

Before you tee off.

Do I need to know how to golf?

Not at all. Our simulators include beginner-friendly modes and mini-games, clubs are provided, and our staff will get you swinging in minutes. Plenty of guests come purely for the party.

How many people fit in a bay?

Each private simulator bay comfortably hosts up to 6 guests with lounge seating, table service, and your own screen.

How do I book?

Reserve online anytime at book.igolf32.com, call us at (646) 838-4004, or email hello@igolf32.com for groups and events.

Where exactly are you?

22 W 32nd St, Floor 6, New York, NY 10001 — in the heart of Koreatown, steps from Herald Square and the 34th St subway hub. Take the elevator to the 6th floor.

Can you host corporate events?

Yes — tournaments, team building, and full-venue buyouts with custom food & beverage packages. Email hello@igolf32.com with your date and headcount.
